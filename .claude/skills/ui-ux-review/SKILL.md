# UI/UX Review Skill

Apply this checklist before accepting any Arcates interface change.

## UX hierarchy
- A visitor should understand what Arcates offers, why it matters and the next action within the first viewport.
- Give every section a distinct purpose; remove duplicated messaging and competing CTAs.
- Primary actions are visually dominant, secondary actions quieter.
- Forms use explicit labels, helpful states and concise error feedback.

## Accessibility
- Semantic landmarks and ordered h1-h3 hierarchy.
- Keyboard-operable controls with visible focus states.
- WCAG AA contrast for body copy and essential controls.
- Touch targets approximately 44px minimum where practical.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion and avoid content hidden permanently when JS/motion is unavailable.

## Responsive review
Validate at 360px, 768px, 1024px and wide desktop. Check navigation, grids, long Turkish copy, CTA wrapping, forms, tables/admin panels and fixed widgets.

## Conversion review
- Place relevant CTA after high-intent sections.
- Reinforce trust with specific process, project and support signals.
- Avoid fake urgency, excessive animation and decorative content that delays the task.
